## Current SSELFIE

SSELFIE is a personal-brand membership and AI content workspace for women building a one-woman brand.
It combines weekly guidance from founder **Sandra Sigurjónsdóttir** with **Maya** and **SSELFIE SUITE**
to help members create the photos, captions, carousels, Stories and planning around their real offer.
